## Доказательства и передача результата

```sql
SELECT statement_timestamp() AT TIME ZONE 'UTC' AS observed_at_utc,
       current_database() AS effective_database,
       current_user AS effective_role,
       pg_is_in_recovery() AS in_recovery,
       inet_server_addr() AS backend_address,
       EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM sales.orders) AS can_read_orders;
```
